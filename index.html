WELCOME TO HCA

More Than a School. A Ministry with Purpose.

Hermanus Christian Academy is first and foremost a ministry. Founded by Dr Nealie and Veronica Ross, the school has served the Hermanus community for more than three decades.

HCA trains children of every ability in Christian leadership, self-discipline, individual responsibility, accountability and personal integrity.
